Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

Account details (name, email or phone, password hash), profile information you choose to add (age, photos, bio, interests), and usage data needed to run matching, chat and payments.

2. How we use it

To create your profile, suggest matches, power chat and notifications, process premium payments through Razorpay, and keep the platform safe from fake or abusive accounts.

3. What we never do

We never sell your personal data to advertisers, and we never ask for or store your Instagram password. Any social login uses the provider's official OAuth flow only.

4. Photos and storage

Profile photos are stored securely and are only visible to other members as governed by your profile and privacy settings.

5. Your controls

You can edit or delete your profile information at any time from Settings, and you can deactivate or permanently delete your account.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact page.
