Why we built LoveLink

Most dating apps optimize for time spent swiping. We built LoveLink to optimize for the opposite — getting you off the app and onto an actual date, or at least a conversation worth having.

That means matching on more than a photo: shared interests, how far apart you are, and what you're actually looking for. It means verified profiles, so the person you're talking to is who they say they are. And it means a chat experience built for real conversation, not just another inbox.

LoveLink is built and run by a small team who believe good product design should make the hard parts of meeting people a little easier — not harder.
